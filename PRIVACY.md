# Privacy Policy for AliExpress Order Master

**Effective Date: March 31, 2026**

This privacy policy governs your use of the "AliExpress Order Master" Chrome extension.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
- **Local Processing:** The extension processes all data locally on your device.
- **Personal Information:** We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).
- **Financial Information:** The extension reads order totals and prices from your active AliExpress tab only to generate the CSV export file as requested by you.
- **Website Content:** The extension fetches product titles from AliExpress in the background to improve accuracy. This data is used only during the active session and is never saved outside your local machine.

### 2. Data Transmission
- **No Remote Servers:** No data is transmitted to any external servers or third parties.
- **Third Parties:** We do not sell, trade, or rent user data to anyone.

### 3. Permissions
- **activeTab/scripting:** Used only to read the order list when you click the extension icon.
- **Host Permissions:** Used solely to fetch English product titles from AliExpress product pages.

### 4. User Consent
By using this extension, you consent to this privacy policy. Since no data is collected, there is no data to delete or manage.

### 5. Contact
If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the GitHub repository.
